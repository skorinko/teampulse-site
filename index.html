Your team's pulse, on your desktop

TeamPulse shows what needs you, what you're waiting on, who is working on what and what's on fire across your GitHub organization: in widgets, the menu bar and a dashboard that fills your desktop.

Coming soon to the Mac App Store

What needs you

Reviews requested from you, your PRs with failed CI and the reason why, merge conflicts, and PRs ready to merge.

What's on fire

CI red on main, force pushes and direct pushes to protected branches, unreviewed merges, hotfixes to production.

Who's on what

Each person's branches, open PRs, tickets and what they shipped, plus the most active repos and people.

For team leads and CTOs

Flow health, DORA delivery metrics, security alerts and CI health, from stale PRs to flaky workflows.

Made for the Mac

Widgets in every size, a menu bar summary, a full-desktop dashboard, notifications, light and dark.

Private

TeamPulse talks only to GitHub. Your sign-in stays in your keychain and your data stays on your Mac. No analytics, no servers of ours.
