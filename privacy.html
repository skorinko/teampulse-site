Privacy Policy

Effective 8 November 2026

In short: TeamPulse reads your GitHub organization's data from GitHub and shows it on your Mac. It sends nothing to the developer, has no servers of its own, and contains no analytics or tracking.

Who we are

TeamPulse for GitHub is published by Dmitry Skorinko, an individual developer, through the Mac App Store.

What the app reads

When you sign in, TeamPulse uses GitHub's API on your behalf to read the organization you choose: pull requests, reviews, checks and Actions runs, branches and pushes, members and teams, and, where your access allows, security alerts and billing usage. It only reads; it never changes anything on GitHub.

Where your data goes

Nowhere but your Mac. TeamPulse connects to:

  • api.github.com and github.com, to sign you in and read your organization's data;
  • githubstatus.com, to read GitHub's public service status.

It makes no other network requests. There is no TeamPulse server, and the developer never receives your data, your sign-in or your usage.

What the app stores

Your GitHub sign-in (an access token) is kept in your Mac's keychain. Your settings and the latest results the app fetched are kept in the app's own container on your Mac, so the widgets can show them. Signing out deletes the token and the fetched results. Deleting the app deletes everything it stored.

Notifications

If you allow them, TeamPulse shows macOS notifications for new urgent items. They are created on your Mac and are not sent through any server.

Sample data

"Try with sample data" shows a made-up organization. It reads nothing from GitHub and needs no account.

GitHub

Your use of GitHub is governed by GitHub's own terms and privacy statement. You can review or revoke TeamPulse's access at any time in your GitHub settings under Applications.

Purchases

TeamPulse is sold through the Mac App Store. Apple handles payment, and the developer does not receive your name, email address or payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to the purchase.

Children

TeamPulse collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
